SHA-256 of ground_truth.json, published before any benchmark run.

The generator is deterministic from a seed. Publishing the seed or the answer
key lets a model reproduce exact glyph frames and coordinates from source
rather than recovering them from the video -- on the first attempt three of
seven runs did exactly that, one scoring a perfect blind L3 without analysing
a single frame. The seed and key are therefore withheld until the runs are
complete, and this hash pins them so they provably were not changed after
seeing results.
